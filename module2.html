Percentage Quest

Theory

Percentage represents a value out of one hundred.

Percentages are commonly used in aptitude problems.

They are useful for marks, discounts, profit and growth.

Important Formulas

Basic Percentage

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

Percentage Increase

Increase Percentage = Increase / Original × 100

Percentage Decrease

Decrease Percentage = Decrease / Original × 100
